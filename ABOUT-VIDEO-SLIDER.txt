ABOUT VIDEO SLIDER

The About page now presents four compact 16:9 video cards in an accessible horizontal carousel.
Use the previous/next buttons, pagination dots, arrow keys, mouse dragging, or touch swipes to navigate.
Click a populated card to play its video in a centered lightbox; close with the X button, Escape, or the backdrop.
Playback supports uploaded/local MP4, M4V, WebM, OGG/OGV and public YouTube/Vimeo links. Videos are not loaded or played on hover.
The carousel respects reduced-motion settings and keeps its visible section labels translated via the existing site locale system.
Upload or choose the four video sources in Admin > About page media and video slots. Each slot also supports an optional poster image URL/path or uploaded poster, displayed lazily before playback.

Navigation controls are overlaid at the left and right edges of the slider. Each arrow or swipe moves exactly one card; slide positioning is calculated from the active card's layout offset so it remains stable in RTL and LTR layouts. Selecting a video opens a viewport-filling lightbox with a blurred page backdrop; close/Escape removes the player to stop playback.
